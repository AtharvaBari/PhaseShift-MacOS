## ✨ The Orbit

**PhaseShift** isn't just a clock; it's a mirror reflecting the finite nature of your journey. It transforms how you perceive time by tracking your year, your age, and your moments with elegance.

| Feature              | Description                                                                 |
| :------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **⏳ Year Progress** | Visualize the passage of the year in real-time. Stay aware, stay motivated. |
| **🎂 Age View**      | A precise memento of your journey, tracked to the decimal.                  |
| **🎨 Customizable**  | Themes, colors, and layouts that adapt to your desktop aesthetic.           |
| **🖥️ Multi-Display** | Works seamlessly across all your connected displays.                        |

## 🚀 Installation

PhaseShift is a native macOS application. Follow these steps to get started:

1.  **Download** the latest **`PhaseShift.dmg`** from the [Releases](https://github.com/AtharvaBari/PhaseShift-MacOS/releases) page.
2.  **Open** the `.dmg` file and **drag** the `PhaseShift` app into your `Applications` folder.
3.  **Launch** it—it will live in your menu bar.

### 🛡️ Security Note

Since this app is open-source and not signed by Apple, you may see a warning:  
_"App cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"_

**To fix this:**

1.  Go to **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security**.
2.  Scroll down to the **Security** section.
3.  Click **"Open Anyway"** for PhaseShift.
